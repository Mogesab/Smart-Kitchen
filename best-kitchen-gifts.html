Tips for gift-giving in the kitchen

  • Consumables are safe bets. Nice olive oil, single-origin spices, quality sea salt - if you're unsure, pair a small gadget with a gourmet consumable.
  • Ask about counter space. The best gift for someone in a 400-square-foot apartment isn't always a stand mixer.
  • Include a gift receipt. Amazon makes returns painless if the recipient already owns it.
