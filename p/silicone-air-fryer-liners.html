Silicone air fryer liners

Reusable silicone liners sit inside the air fryer basket. They catch drips and prevent sticking, then peel out for a quick rinse. Way less waste than parchment squares.

Why this can be a smart pick

  • No more scrubbing baked-on cheese or marinade
  • Dishwasher safe

What to check before you buy

  • MUST match your basket shape - square vs round
  • Check heat rating (400 F+)
  • Small holes matter for air circulation

Great for

  • Reheating leftovers
  • Sticky glazed anything

Frequently asked questions

Do they affect crisping?

Slightly - food on the liner crisps a hair less on the bottom. For most meals it is worth the cleanup savings.
