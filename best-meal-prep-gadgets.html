Meal-prep routines that actually stick

  • Prep only what you'll eat in 4 days. Cooked food quality drops fast after that unless it's frozen.
  • Label everything. A date-label dispenser turns "is this still okay?" into a 2-second decision.
  • Freeze in single portions. A vacuum sealer or silicone bags do this dramatically better than plastic wrap.
  • Buy identical containers. Uniform shapes stack. Random tupperware doesn't.
